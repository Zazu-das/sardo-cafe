# QR Code Menu Access Guide\n\n## 📱 How the QR System Works\n\n### For Customers:\n\n1. **Scan QR Code** - Customer scans QR code on their table\n2. **Menu Opens** - Digital menu loads in their web browser (no app needed!)\n3. **Browse & Order** - Customer selects items and places order\n4. **Confirmation** - Order appears instantly on staff dashboard\n5. **Wait** - Staff updates order status as it's prepared\n\n---\n\n## 🖨️ For Staff: Generating & Printing QR Codes\n\n### Step 1: Access QR Generator\n1. Open Staff Dashboard at `http://localhost:3000`\n2. Click **\"📱 QR Codes\"** tab\n\n### Step 2: Choose QR Size\n- **Small** (200x200px) - For small tables\n- **Medium** (300x300px) - Standard size\n- **Large** (400x400px) - Easy to scan from distance\n\n### Step 3: Select Tables\n- Click individual table numbers to select\n- Use **\"Select All\"** to generate for all 19 tables\n- Checkmarks appear on selected tables\n\n### Step 4: Print or Download\n\n**Option A: Print Individual QR Codes**\n- Click the 🖨️ button on each table card\n- Prints single QR code\n- Scale as needed\n\n**Option B: Print All Selected**\n- Click **\"🖨️ Print Selected\"**\n- Opens print preview\n- Prints in 2x2 grid format\n- Professional layout ready for framing\n\n**Option C: Download as Images**\n- Click **\"⬇️ Download\"** on individual tables\n- Saves as PNG file\n- Use in marketing materials\n- Or click **\"⬇️ Download All\"** for batch download\n\n---\n\n## 🎯 Setup Instructions\n\n### After Generating QR Codes:\n\n1. **Print QR Codes**\n   - Use color printer for best results\n   - Recommended: Photo-quality paper or sticker sheets\n\n2. **Mount on Tables**\n   - Attach to table edge or center\n   - Face outward so customer can easily scan\n   - Use laminate sheet to protect from spills\n\n3. **Test It**\n   - Scan QR code with phone\n   - Verify menu loads correctly\n   - Place a test order\n\n4. **Share URL** (optional)\n   - You can also share menu URL directly\n   - Example: `https://yourdomain.com/menu?table=5`\n   - Print on receipt or table card\n\n---\n\n## 🔗 QR Code Details\n\n### What's in the QR Code?\n- Points to your menu web app\n- Includes table number automatically\n- No internet required after page loads\n- Works offline if browser cached\n\n### Example QR Code URL:\n```\nhttps://yourdomain.com/menu?table=5\n```\n\n---\n\n## 📧 Sharing Menu with Customers\n\n### Method 1: QR Codes (Recommended)\n- Most convenient\n- No typing required\n- Professional appearance\n\n### Method 2: Direct Link\n- Share URL on social media\n- Include in email/messaging\n- Print on receipt\n\n### Method 3: NFC Tags\n- Future enhancement\n- More stable than QR codes\n- Single scan opens menu\n\n---\n\n## 🔧 Troubleshooting\n\n| Issue | Solution |\n|-------|----------|\n| QR code won't scan | Check camera focus, ensure good lighting |\n| Menu won't load | Check internet connection, verify API is running |\n| Wrong table number | Regenerate QR code with correct table number |\n| QR code blurry when printed | Try larger size (Medium or Large) |\n| Menu loads but doesn't connect to orders | Ensure backend is running on correct port |\n\n---\n\n## 💡 Pro Tips\n\n✅ **Generate QR codes for each table**\n- Makes ordering process smooth\n- Reduces confusion about table numbers\n\n✅ **Print QR codes on sticker sheets**\n- Easy to replace if damaged\n- Professional appearance\n- Weatherproof options available\n\n✅ **Laminate QR codes**\n- Protects from spills\n- Increases lifespan\n- Still scans through plastic\n\n✅ **Create QR code frames**\n- Print with \"Scan to Order\" text\n- Add SARDO Café logo\n- Makes it clear what to do\n\n✅ **Test before deployment**\n- Scan with different phone models\n- Test from different distances\n- Verify all functions work\n\n---\n\n## 📊 Stats & Monitoring\n\nFuture features:\n- Track which tables scan most\n-
